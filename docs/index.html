Privacy Policy

App: VideosMerge (影剪) · Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Contact: 446741151@qq.com

This Privacy Policy describes how VideosMerge ("the App", "we") handles information when you use our iOS application. We designed the App to process your videos locally on your device and we operate no servers that store your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Videos and edited content. All videos you import, parse, edit, merge, or export are processed and stored only on your device. We never upload your media to our servers (we have none).
  • Information collected by Apple. App Store analytics (downloads, crashes, usage statistics) are collected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Information collected by advertising partners. The App may display ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalization in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking, or Google Ads Settings.

2. Permissions the App Requests

  • Photo Library — to import videos and save exported results to your photo library. Media is accessed only on your instruction.
  • Local Network / Internet — to download videos from links you explicitly paste into the App, and to load ads and app configuration.

3. Third-Party Link Parsing

When you paste a share link into the App, the App contacts the relevant third-party platform (or its public endpoints) to retrieve the video you requested. These requests contain only the link you provided. We do not intercept, log, or store them, because we operate no backend. Your use of third-party platforms remains subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Tracking

The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If iOS shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, it relates solely to allowing advertising partners to deliver more relevant ads; declining does not reduce the App's functionality.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we do not maintain user accounts or databases, the data available to us is limited to what Apple and Google collect as described above; you may exercise your rights with those providers directly, or contact us by email.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the App's next update.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: 446741151@qq.com

隐私政策

应用：影剪（VideosMerge） · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 12 日 · 联系邮箱：446741151@qq.com

本政策说明影剪（"本应用"、"我们"）如何处理信息。本应用的全部视频处理均在你的设备本地完成，我们不运营任何存储个人数据的服务器。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 视频与导出内容：你导入、解析、编辑、合并、导出的视频只在设备上处理和存储，绝不上传到我们的服务器（我们没有服务器）。
  • 苹果收集的数据：App Store 分析（下载、崩溃、使用统计）由苹果收集，适用苹果隐私政策。
  • 广告合作方收集的数据：本应用可能展示由 Google AdMob 投放的广告。谷歌可能收集设备标识符、IP 地址与使用数据，详见谷歌隐私政策。你可在 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 中限制广告个性化。

2. 应用申请的权限

  • 照片图库：用于导入视频和保存导出结果，仅在你主动操作时访问。
  • 网络：用于下载你主动粘贴链接对应的视频、加载广告与应用配置。

3. 第三方链接解析

当你粘贴分享链接时，本应用会联系相关第三方平台（或其公开接口）以获取你请求的视频。请求中仅包含你提供的链接。由于我们没有后端，不会拦截、记录或存储这些请求。你对第三方平台的使用受其自身条款与隐私政策约束。

4. 跟踪

本应用不会跨应用、跨网站跟踪你。若 iOS 出现"允许跟踪"弹窗，仅与广告个性化有关，拒绝不影响任何功能。

5. 未成年人

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

根据所在司法辖区（如 GDPR、CCPA），你可能有权访问、更正或删除个人数据。由于我们不维护用户账户或数据库，可获得的数据仅限于上文所述苹果与谷歌收集的部分，请直接向其行使权利，或通过邮箱联系我们。
